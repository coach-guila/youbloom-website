My Story

Built, stumbled,
rediscovered.

As an Architect for nearly 30 years, I built things — buildings, careers, companies, a family, a life. From architecture to sustainability consulting and everything in between. But somewhere along the way, I hit a wall. Burned out. Pop Down, as we say in Jamaica.

So, I paused. And in that pause, I unearthed another chapter — as a Certified Professional Life Coach. Your Architect of Wellbeing.

Now in my 50's, I've built, stumbled, pivoted, rebuilt, and rediscovered myself. And I help others do the same.
